Clear Space Rule

Always maintain a minimum clear space equal to the height of the "S" letterform around all sides of the logo. Never crowd the logo with other elements, text, or edges.

Logo Don'ts
Don't stretch

Never distort or change the proportions of the logo in any direction.

Don't recolor

Never use colors outside the brand palette for the logo or icon mark.

Don't add effects

No drop shadows, glows, bevels, or gradients applied to the logo.

Don't rotate

The logo must always appear horizontal. No angled or rotated placements.

Don't use on busy BG

Never place the logo on complex imagery without a clean overlay or background.

Don't alter text

Never change the font, spacing, or layout of the wordmark or tagline.

Tone of Voice

Seamless FM speaks like an expert who gets things done. We're direct, confident, and solution-focused — not corporate-stiff. We respect our clients' time and speak to results, not process.

Authoritative
We know facility management. Our voice demonstrates deep expertise without being condescending.
"Multi-site HVAC requires a system — not a series of one-off calls."
Direct
No fluff. State the benefit, the action, or the result. Short sentences win.
"Fast response. Qualified techs. Done right."
Professional
We serve commercial clients. Language is polished, grammar is tight, tone is business-appropriate.
"Our preventative maintenance programs reduce unexpected downtime by up to 40%."
Trustworthy
Every promise we make is one we can keep. Reliability is the core of how we communicate.
"We'll take it from there. Every time."
Results-Driven
Lead with outcomes. Clients care about what happens, not how we do it.
"Refer a client. Earn $500 on the first call, $2,000 on first order."
Approachable
Professional but human. We're partners, not vendors. A little warmth goes a long way.
"Know a business that needs us? We'll handle the rest."
Don't write this
"We are pleased to offer a comprehensive suite of integrated facility management solutions..."
"Our team of professionals leverages best-in-class methodologies..."
"Please do not hesitate to contact us at your earliest convenience."
"We provide value-added services across multiple verticals."
Write this instead
"We handle HVAC, plumbing, electrical — across all your locations."
"Certified techs. Fast response. No excuses."
"Call us today at (267) 814-5880 — we'll take it from there."
"One call covers all your commercial facilities."
